How it works

Saved scenarios and share links live in your browser. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

The roles, skills and training lengths are made-up examples to show how it works. A real version would use local listings and approved programs.

It does not measure emissions.

The money check is a weekly estimate with no taxes or severance. The $450 for 26 weeks is a placeholder for your state's benefit.
